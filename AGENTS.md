# Learner-facing Japanese text

This website is for Thai beginners who cannot yet read Japanese. Always include Romaji alongside Japanese words and sentences in explanations, tips, notes, headings, and examples. In prose, use `これ (kore)` and `このお弁当 (kono obentou)`; for a group, annotate each term separately. Learning cards and dialogue may put Romaji on an immediately adjacent line. Thai readings complement Romaji and do not replace it. Preserve long vowels and particle pronunciation (は → wa, を → o). Keep this rule when adding or editing content.
